Stockscribes

A stock-ordering platform for pharmacies and the wholesalers who supply them.

The problem

Pharmacies in Nigeria order stock by phone. Every restock means calling each supplier to ask what's available and what it costs — with no way to compare prices, and no record afterwards.

How it works

Two kinds of users share one live catalogue. Pharmacy staff search, compare prices across suppliers and order in a few clicks. Suppliers sign in to keep their own prices, stock and expiry dates current, and to confirm incoming orders — so the pharmacy always sees today's information without picking up the phone.

Try it — two minutes

  1. Sign in as the pharmacy and search paracetamol. Three suppliers, cheapest marked — note that it's the short-dated one, which is how pricing really works.
  2. Add something to the basket and place an order. It splits by supplier automatically.
  3. Open a private browser window, sign in as the supplier, change a price or mark an item out of stock, then refresh the pharmacy window.
